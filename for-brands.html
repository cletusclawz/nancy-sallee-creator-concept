Campaign formats to discuss.

Position these as flexible collaboration concepts, not unverified promises. Final scope, rates, usage rights and timelines belong in the signed agreement.

01 · EDUCATE

Beginner tutorials

Clear language around color, cap construction, density, fit and care for shoppers still learning.

02 · DEMONSTRATE

Try-ons and styling

Product-led short-form content that shows how a wig looks, moves and can be styled in real life.

03 · REVIEW

Honest product stories

Balanced reviews that answer the practical questions buyers ask before committing.

04 · CONNECT

Confidence narratives

Human stories that connect alternative hair with identity, comfort and the freedom to feel like yourself.

05 · CONVERT

Shopping-path content

Campaign landing paths that make the offer, disclosure, code and next action easier to find.

06 · EXTEND

Usage-ready UGC

Brand-owned creative can be discussed separately, with explicit usage period, placements and rights.
